Three homepage directions

These are concept mockups for a site built from different chapters or corners of you, not one strict life timeline. Each one has the same core sections: Life Story, Constitution, Work, and Library.

Compare the vibes
Editorial Enchanted Minimal Index of Rooms

Mockup 1: Editorial

Cleanest and chicest. The whimsy is in the language and pacing, not decoration.

Most minimal

Laura Vandendorpe

A small private index.

Not a chronology, exactly. More like a set of cleanly arranged entries: the story, the constitution, the work, the books, the self in chapters.

Life Story Constitution Work Library

Life Story

Eras, turning points, sidequests, and the mood of each chapter.

Constitution

Rules, contradictions, standards, beliefs, tastes, and private doctrine.

Work

Ambition, projects, what you build, how you think, and what you want next.

Library

Books read, books loved, books returned to when you want your bearings back.

Why it works

It feels expensive, self-possessed, and literary without trying too hard.

Best for

If you want people to feel like they’ve entered a very curated personal archive.

Risk

It may be a little too restrained if you want the homepage to feel overtly magical.

Mockup 2: Enchanted Minimal

My recommendation

Enchanted Minimal is probably the best fit if you want whimsical and cool at the same time. Editorial is strongest if you want something more timeless and chic.

What we’d do next

Pick one direction, then I’ll turn it into your real homepage and make the four chapter entries feel specifically yours.

Possible extra chapters
